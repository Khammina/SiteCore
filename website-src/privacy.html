Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026 · Applies to the SiteCore Android app, the SiteCore web app and SiteCore desktop file.

In short: SiteCore does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. There are no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. Everything you enter stays on your own device unless you choose to share it.

1. Who we are

SiteCore is a field quality and punch list application created by Khammina SCMV ("we", "us"). Contact details are at the end of this policy.

2. Information the app handles

To work, SiteCore stores the information you enter, only in the app's private storage on your device (Android app) or in your browser's storage (web app):

  • Project details, company names and logos you add.
  • Drawings / layout plans you import (PDF or image files).
  • Tags, punch list items, comments, inspections, site diary entries, and the names of people you type in (for example "assigned to").
  • Photos you take or choose, including any markup you draw.
  • App preferences such as language and display size.

This information is not transmitted to us or to any third party. We have no server that receives it and we cannot see it.

3. Device permissions

PermissionWhy it is used
Camera (optional)Only when you choose to take a photo for a tag, diary entry or logo. Photos are saved inside the app on your device.
Files / media pickerOnly when you choose a drawing, photo, logo or project file to import. The system picker gives the app access to that file only.
Storage (Android 10 and older only)To save a copy of the reports and files you export in the Documents/SiteCore folder.
InternetRequired by the Android system component that displays the app. SiteCore does not send your data over the internet.

4. Sharing

Reports (PDF), spreadsheets (CSV), project packages and backups leave your device only when you choose Share, Download or Export. You decide the destination (for example e-mail, a messaging app or cloud storage). Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Your data remains on your device until you delete it.
  • Delete a single project in Project Settings › Delete this project.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the Android app, or by clearing the site data for SiteCore in your browser.
  • If you use Android's device backup, the system may include the app's data in your personal Google backup, under Google's terms. You can turn this off in Android settings.

6. Children

SiteCore is a professional tool for the construction industry. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Keep your device protected with a screen lock, and store exported files and backups in a secure place.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
