Features

Everything the site team needs

From walk-down to handover: capture issues where they are, assign them, follow them up and report them professionally.

Drawings & tags

Import PDF or image plans and pin issues exactly where they are. Organise as Discipline › Floor › Sub-system › Layout.

Punch lists

Status, priority, responsible company, due dates, comments and closed dates — with filters and bulk updates.

Photos & markup

Take photos on site, draw arrows, boxes and notes, and label them Before / After.

Inspections

Pass / Fail / N/A checklists from templates. Failed items become tags on the plan in one tap.

Task board & diary

Track responsibilities between teams and keep a daily site diary with manpower, weather and photos.

Share & back up

Send a project to the office or field team as one file, and back up all projects to your own storage.

PDF reports

Reports your client will sign off

  • Owner, consultant and contractor logos in the header
  • Layout overview with every tag, then detail pages with photos and key plan
  • Report any level — from a single sub-system to the whole discipline
  • Preview, adjust and print in English, Lao or Thai
  • Filter by responsible company for subcontractor reports

FAQ

Questions

Does SiteCore work without internet?

Yes. The Android app and the installed web app work fully offline. Your data is stored on the device.

Where is my data stored? Who can see it?

Only on your device. SiteCore has no server and does not collect any data. Read the privacy policy.

How do I share a project with the office?

Use Share / Export to create one project file, send it by e-mail, WhatsApp or Drive, and use Import on the other device.

Which languages are supported?

English, Lao and Thai — for the app and for the PDF reports, independently.

Can I use my company logo?

Yes. Each project has three header logos — owner, consultant and contractor — with editable captions.
